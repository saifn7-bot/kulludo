KulLUDO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

KulLUDO ("the app", "we") is a Ludo board game for Android, iOS and the web, published by Mohammed Saif. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: we do not collect personal information, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves a few things only on your own device: your sound setting, your chosen colour and nickname, an unfinished offline game so you can resume it, and a random player ID used to reconnect you to an online room. This information never leaves your device except as described in section 2, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

2. Online games

When you choose Play Online, the app connects to our game server (hosted on Cloudflare) and sends the nickname you typed, your random player ID and your game moves. The server uses this only to run the game room, shows your nickname to the other players in that room, and does not store it permanently: a room and everything in it is deleted when all players leave. Emoji reactions you send are shown to other players in the room and are not stored.

Like any internet service, our hosting provider Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the connection and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. What we do not do

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, sell or share data, or offer in-app purchases.

4. Permissions

The app only uses internet access (for online games) and vibration (for game feedback).

5. Children

KulLUDO does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Nicknames are chosen freely by players; please do not use your real full name as a nickname.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
