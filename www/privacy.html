Privacy Policy

KulLUDO · Effective 28 October 2026

In short: KulLUDO has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We don't ask for your name, email, phone, location or contacts. Online games only use the nickname you type, and it is deleted when the game room closes.

1. Who we are

KulLUDO (the "App") is published by Trade Matrix Technologies and Trading, a proprietorship of Mohammed Saif, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). We are the data fiduciary / data controller for the limited information described below.

2. Information kept only on your device

The App stores on your own device: your sound setting, chosen colour and nickname, an unfinished offline game (so you can resume it), players you have muted, your acceptance of our Terms, and a random player ID used to reconnect you to an online room. This stays on your device; you can delete it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

3. Information used for online games

WhatYour nickname, random player ID, game moves and emoji reactions
WhyOnly to run the online game room (App functionality)
Who sees itThe other players in that room
How longHeld in server memory while the room is active; deleted when all players leave. Not written to any database.
Legal basisYour consent and our legitimate interest in providing the game you asked to play

Nicknames are automatically checked for offensive words. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, Inc., processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver connections and protect against abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Data may be processed on Cloudflare servers outside your country.

4. Reports you send us

If you report a player or contact us by email, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to handle your request. We keep it for up to 12 months, then delete it.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell or share personal data, show ads, use analytics, advertising IDs or tracking, build profiles, or offer purchases. The App uses only internet access (online games) and vibration (game feedback).

6. Children

KulLUDO is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Players should never use their real full name as a nickname.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. Because we keep almost no data, most rights are met by clearing the App's data; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

All online traffic is encrypted (HTTPS / secure WebSocket). No method is 100% secure, but we keep data to the minimum needed.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's store listing.
